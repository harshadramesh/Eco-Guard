How the prediction works

Transparent statistical estimate, not a trained AI model

The prototype fits a straight-line trend to the latest seven daily readings and projects the next 30 days. A short history can be noisy, so treat the result as an early planning signal—not a guaranteed forecast.

STATISTICAL MONITORING

AI-powered anomaly detection

Find readings that are unusually high compared with recent history and expected baselines.

Adaptive detection method: EcoGuard compares each new reading with its recent historical average and standard deviation. When enough timestamped samples are available, it prefers readings from the same hour of day. Inspection feedback adjusts each zone/resource alert sensitivity: confirmed faults lower the deviation limit, while no-fault inspections raise it. This is transparent statistical detection—not a trained AI model. An alert is a signal to investigate, not proof of a fault.

Resolution checklist

  • Inspect AC units, lighting and equipment schedules.
  • Check taps, flush valves, tanks and visible pipework.
  • Record the corrective action and completion time.
  • Take a new reading and compare it with the previous reading.

When maintenance staff finish an inspection, record the actual finding and notes. EcoGuard stores the outcome and adjusts future anomaly sensitivity for that zone and resource. The demo does not pretend that an inspection or repair happened automatically.

SMARTER RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

Small signals.
Meaningful savings.

EcoGuard is a browser-based campus resource monitoring concept that helps administrators spot unusual electricity and water consumption before wastage goes unnoticed.

What EcoGuard does

It simulates readings for campus buildings and hostel blocks, compares them with adaptive historical patterns, visualizes trends and creates reviewable alerts.

Technology stack

HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript ES6+ · Chart.js 4 · Browser LocalStorage. No paid API, physical sensor or external database is required for this demo.

Important limitation

This hackathon demo uses simulated data. An alert means a reading is statistically unusual compared with historical patterns; it does not prove that a tap, pipe or appliance is physically leaking or faulty.

Next steps

A production deployment could connect verified smart meters, role-based access, secure server storage, notification channels and more advanced forecasting.
